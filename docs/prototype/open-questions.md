# Methodology questions to confirm

These questions should be answered before this prototype becomes a production calculator.

1. Does “lead” always mean a workshop registration, or are reach, landing-page visitors, registrations and launch-list members separate funnel stages?
2. Which rounding rules should be authoritative for attendance, spend and revenue beyond the whole-client and whole-registration rules already confirmed?
3. For Complete or another advanced flow, when a group exists but no historical join rate is available, should 30% be prefilled or shown only as a benchmark? What lower reference, if any, applies outside Facebook? Separately, should community participation ever adjust the sales-conversion case, and if so, what evidence-backed magnitude and conversion base apply? Beginner now omits community planning.
4. What should happen at exactly €297, and how should the threshold work outside EUR?
5. Should Beginner’s audience-research questions remain on Screen 6, move to Screen 1 or be removed? Community planning is now omitted, but the research screen’s placement remains unresolved.
6. Which invite-email schedule is authoritative: the four-email sequence on p. 19 or the five-email sequence on p. 24?
7. At exactly 3% post-launch conversion, should the client receive an upsell, downsell, neither, or coach review?
8. Is cost per paid registration entered manually, sourced from prior launches, or estimated by a separate model?
9. After EUR is preselected and currency choice is secondary, which non-EUR currencies, if any, should be pinned before “More currencies”?
10. What defines a bigger or older email list, and which lower signup rates should be suggested? Should list age, engagement or audience warmth control the guidance?
11. Should an email-list registration estimate round to the nearest whole person, down, or up?
12. Which five historical launches should form the first validation set, and what inputs, outputs, recommendation, red flags and coach override does Sigrun approve for each?

Resolved decisions are recorded in [the Phase 0 validation log](validation-log.md). The interface sends unresolved methodology choices to coach review instead of inventing an answer.
