# Methodology questions to confirm

These questions should be answered before this prototype becomes a production calculator.

1. Does the 1–3% sales conversion apply to everyone registered for the workshop or only to people who attend live?
2. Does “lead” always mean a workshop registration, or are reach, landing-page visitors, registrations and launch-list members separate funnel stages?
3. Which rounding rules should be authoritative for attendance, spend and revenue beyond the whole-client and whole-registration rules already confirmed?
4. Should 60% remain a useful workshop-group joining reference, and when should the 70% historical example be used?
5. What should happen at exactly €297, and how should the threshold work outside EUR?
6. Is an online group required for three-day workshops and optional for one-day workshops, or can another community space be used?
7. Which invite-email schedule is authoritative: the four-email sequence on p. 19 or the five-email sequence on p. 24?
8. At exactly 3% post-launch conversion, should the client receive an upsell, downsell, neither, or coach review?
9. Is cost per paid registration entered manually, sourced from prior launches, or estimated by a separate model?
10. Which offer types, currencies and launch archetypes must the first pilot support?
11. Should the email signup percentage stay entirely user-selected, or should list-size bands suggest lower starting rates? If bands are wanted, what are the thresholds and rates?
12. Should an email-list registration estimate round to the nearest whole person, down, or up?

Resolved decisions are recorded in [the Phase 0 validation log](validation-log.md). The interface sends unresolved methodology choices to coach review instead of inventing an answer.
