# Methodology questions to confirm

These questions should be answered before this prototype becomes a production calculator.

1. Can we obtain the Launch Calculator linked from Module 2, Lesson 1, or its current formula specification?
2. Does “lead” always mean a workshop registration, or are reach, landing-page visitors, registrations and launch-list members separate funnel stages?
3. Which rounding rules should be authoritative for buyers, registrations, attendance, spend and revenue?
4. Is the general 20–30% attendance range still current, and when should a stronger historical rate be used?
5. Should 60% remain the planning default for workshop-group joining, despite the 70% historical example?
6. What should happen at exactly €297, and how should the threshold work outside EUR?
7. Is a Facebook group required for three-day workshops and optional for one-day workshops?
8. Which invite-email schedule is authoritative: the four-email sequence on p. 19 or the five-email sequence on p. 24?
9. How should “small audience” be defined around the directional 500-email / 1,000-social thresholds?
10. At exactly 3% post-launch conversion, should the client receive an upsell, downsell, neither, or coach review?
11. Is cost per paid registration entered manually, sourced from prior launches, or estimated by a separate model?
12. Which offer types, currencies and launch archetypes must the first pilot support?

Until confirmed, the user interface labels these areas as working assumptions or coach decisions.
